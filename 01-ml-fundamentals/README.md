## Start here
- StatQuest on YouTube ([channel](https://www.youtube.com/@statquest)): *Machine Learning Fundamentals: Bias and Variance*, *Cross Validation*, *ROC and AUC, Clearly Explained*, *Sensitivity and Specificity*
- Google ML Crash Course: [Overfitting](https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/crash-course/overfitting) and [Classification metrics](https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/crash-course/classification)
- scikit-learn: [Cross-validation](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/cross_validation.html) · [Model evaluation](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/model_evaluation.html) · [Common pitfalls (leakage)](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/common_pitfalls.html)
- Kaggle Learn: [Data leakage lesson](https://www.kaggle.com/code/alexisbcook/data-leakage)

## Go deeper
- ISLP ([free PDF](https://www.statlearning.com/)): ch. 2 (bias–variance trade-off), ch. 5 (resampling), ch. 6 (regularisation)
- scikit-learn: [Validation and learning curves](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/learning_curve.html) · [Probability calibration](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/calibration.html)
- Kapoor & Narayanan, [Leakage and the reproducibility crisis in ML-based science](https://reproducible.cs.princeton.edu/)
